# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Lead defines quality standards, owns the test strategy, and ensures that features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release. They work closely with Developers and Product Managers to validate that deliverables meet quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain comprehensive test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain automated and manual test cases
- Coordinate QA execution and regression testing across sprints
- Validate acceptance criteria before PR/issue closure
- Identify, triage, and track quality issues
- Provide input on Definition of Done and quality metrics
- Collaborate with Developers on test strategy and coverage

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Reduce cycle time for quality validation
- Provide clear quality metrics and dashboards
- Build quality into the development process, not just at the end

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Partner on test strategy, code review for testability, and fixing defects
- **With Product Managers:** Validate acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **With Project Managers:** Report on quality status and escalate blockers

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- QA sign-off on PRs and issues
- Quality reports and metrics dashboards
- Test strategy discussions and retrospectives

---

## Product Lead / Director

### Role Summary
Product Lead provides strategic oversight, mentorship, and organizational alignment for product initiatives. They ensure team decisions align with broader business goals and develop leadership capability across Product Managers and Project Managers.

### Responsibilities
- Mentor Product Managers and Project Managers in strategy and execution
- Review and approve project charters and roadmaps
- Align projects with organizational strategy and resources
- Escalate and resolve cross-team dependencies
- Lead stakeholder alignment and sponsorship reviews
- Provide guidance on trade-offs and resource allocation

### Goals
- Maintain strategic alignment across projects
- Develop team leadership capability
- Ensure efficient use of organizational resources
- Maximize organizational impact and product portfolio health

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers:** Mentorship, roadmap alignment, and strategic guidance
- **With Project Managers:** Leadership development and escalation authority
- **With Developers & QA:** Facilitate alignment on technical feasibility and quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder reviews
- Bi-weekly alignment with PMs and Project Managers
- Ad-hoc escalation and guidance
- Strategic planning sessions and reviews

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsor provides business authority, resources, and governance oversight for strategic initiatives. They represent the business perspective and enable projects to succeed by removing organizational barriers and providing decision-making authority.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters, budgets, and resource allocation
- Provide business context and define success from a strategic perspective
- Escalate and resolve level-3 blockers and risks
- Authorize scope changes, timeline adjustments, and trade-offs
- Communicate project status and value to external stakeholders
- Support team decision-making with executive authority

### Goals
- Ensure business value delivery and ROI
- Maintain governance and compliance
- Support team decision-making with authority and remove organizational obstacles
- Align project outcomes with business strategy

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Leads:** Strategic alignment and resource decisions
- **With Project Managers:** Escalation point for major blockers and approvals
- **With Product Managers:** Business context and success definition

### Typical Communication
- Monthly steering committee reviews
- Escalation meetings for level-3 issues
- Approval of major scope/timeline changes
- Executive updates on progress and risk

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Lead provides architectural guidance, manages technical dependencies, and ensures solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical standards. They work closely with Developers to ensure quality implementation and help identify technical risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design standards
- Guide technical decisions and design reviews
- Manage technical dependencies and integration points
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and performance concerns
- Mentor Developers on best practices and code quality
- Collaborate on estimation and feasibility assessment
- Ensure solutions meet scalability, security, and maintainability requirements

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable high-velocity development through clear architecture
- Build team technical capability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers:** Technical mentorship, design reviews, and guidance
- **With Product Managers:** Feasibility assessment and technical trade-offs
- **With QA/Testing Lead:** Technical strategy for testing and quality gates

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Technical documentation and standards
- Code review sessions and mentoring

---

## Scrum Master / Process Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Master / Process Facilitator removes blockers, facilitates team ceremonies, and coaches the team on process adherence. They enable the team to work efficiently by managing process workflows and fostering continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on Agile/Scrum practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain project board and workflow status
- Support collaboration and psychological safety within the team
- Track and escalate process-level risks and improvements

### Goals
- Enable high-velocity, sustainable team performance
- Foster continuous improvement and learning culture
- Maintain process clarity and team alignment
- Reduce time spent on process overhead

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers:** Support in scheduling, facilitation, and escalation
- **With All Roles:** Facilitate ceremonies, remove blockers, and enable collaboration
- **With Team Members:** Coach on process and foster psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and retrospectives
- Daily standups and blocker resolution
- Process improvement discussions
- Team health check-ins and feedback sessions
